Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Track the habits you want to build and the ones you want to leave behind, your way.

Habits your way

Pick an icon, a color and an animation. Repeat on chosen weekdays or any 3 times a week, once a day or several times, like 8 glasses of water.

Routines

Group your day into Morning, Afternoon and Evening, and drag your habits into the order you like.

Timers

For habits measured in minutes, like reading 30 minutes a day. Start and pause from the list or your wrist, and get notified when you're done.

Habits to quit

Smoking, sugar, late-night scrolling: count the days clean since your last slip.

Reminders

One a day, or spread through the day ("3 of 8"), with Done and Check In buttons right in the notification.

Days off

Skip a sick day, pause a habit, or pause them all for a vacation, without breaking your streaks.

Statistics

Completion rate, streaks, a consistency heatmap, your best weekdays, and a year calendar for every habit.

Milestones

Celebrate 7, 30 and 100-day streaks, and share a streak as an image card.

Siri and Shortcuts

"Check in Drink Water in HabitTracker", or ask what's left for today. Works in Spotlight and with the Action button too.

iPad and Mac

Your habits in a sidebar, with each habit's statistics beside them, and keyboard shortcuts on the Mac.

Your data, your way

Export everything to CSV for a spreadsheet, and back up and restore through the Files app.

Make it yours

Six app icons, and the app in English, Italiano, Français or Español, whatever your device's language.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, Mac with macOS 26 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later, paired with an iPhone.

Can I contact support from the app?

Yes: Settings › Support › Contact Support writes an email with details that help us find a problem, like the app version, your device and iOS version. It never includes your habits' names, notes or places, and you see everything before you send it.

How do I move my habits to a new iPhone?

In Settings › Backup, choose Back Up to Files and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new iPhone, like iCloud Drive. Then, on the new iPhone, choose Restore from Backup.

Can I skip a day without losing my streak?

Yes. Swipe a habit and choose Skip Today, turn on Paused when editing a habit, or use Settings › Vacation to pause all of them. Paused days never count as missed.
