Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

HabitTracker doesn't collect, keep or share any of your data. What you track stays on your devices.

What we collect

Nothing. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code, and the app doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else. If you've chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

Your habits stay on your devices

Your habits, check-ins, day notes, places and settings are stored only on your device. The Apple Watch app gets them straight from your iPhone, over Apple's connection between the two, not through a server.

Location

HabitTracker uses your location only if you add a place to a habit with Current Location, and then just once, with your permission. The place is saved with the habit on your device. Searching for a place uses Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your device, which shows them at their time. They don't come from a server.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you use HabitTracker with Siri, Shortcuts or Spotlight, Apple handles your request, under Apple's privacy policy. HabitTracker doesn't receive anything more.

Backups and exports

Backups and CSV exports are files you create, saved where you choose, like the Files app or iCloud Drive. HabitTracker doesn't send them anywhere.

Emails to support

If you write to support from the app (Settings › Support › Contact Support), the email includes the app's version, your device model and system version, your language, region and time zone, whether notifications and location are allowed, and how many habits you have. It never includes your habits' names, notes or places, and you see the whole email before you send it. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems.

Children

HabitTracker doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new version, with its date at the top.

Questions about your privacy? Write to us: support@example.com
